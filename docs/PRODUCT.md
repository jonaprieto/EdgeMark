# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One technical person, a developer or researcher, who captures and reviews their own day:
meals, sleep, ideas, todos, meetings and what they did for work, personal life or a project.
They write and dictate in Spanish and English, often one-handed, walking or in a car, and
they are at home with GitHub and Markdown. Other people, Android users among them, come later
and should find the same app without a setup guide.

## Product Purpose

EdgeMark keeps notes and a daily journal as plain Markdown in the user's own private GitHub
repository, with no server and no account. On the iPhone the job is capture in seconds and a
glance at what a day held; on the Mac it is a side panel for reading and editing. Success is a
capture that takes less time than deciding whether to write it down, and a past day that
reads at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, private. Quiet surfaces; exact information; color only where it carries
meaning, such as a time, a tag, a place or a piece of code. The interface never talks about
itself.

## Anti-references

- A social feed: avatars, likes, comments, anything that suggests an audience.
- A SaaS analytics dashboard: KPI cards, charts for their own sake.
- A gamified habit tracker: streaks, confetti, achievement badges.

## Design Principles

- Capture first: the primary action on any screen is one tap or one sentence away.
- The file is the truth: what the app shows is what the Markdown says, drawn better; nothing
  that only exists in the app.
- Meaning earns color: times, tags, places and code get badges because they are data; prose
  stays plain.
- Native before custom: system controls, Dynamic Type and the platform's own patterns, unless a
  custom drawing says something the system cannot.
- Private by default: say where data goes, once, in plain words.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in every theme, Dynamic Type respected, VoiceOver labels on every icon
button, reduced motion honored. Spanish and English throughout, as dictated.
